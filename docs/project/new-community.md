# New community

Follow these steps to open a community with its own currency on komunitin.org.

## Do you really want to open a new community?

To join an existing community, find it on [komunitin.org](https://komunitin.org). To evaluate the system, use the [Demo Site](../overview/demo.md).

Before applying, define the community's objectives, participation rules, currency unit and administration responsibilities. Identify the responsible organisation or representatives. Read the [Terms of use and legal notice](terms-of-use.md), [Privacy policy](privacy.md), and the maintenance and data arrangements below.

## Import data

For a community already using another system, [contact the platform operator](contact.md) before applying to discuss importing membership and exchange history.

## Apply

Complete the application with the community name, description, contact details, currency name and symbol. Choose a four-letter uppercase code: it identifies the community and forms the prefix of its account numbers.

After approval, the community administrator can configure the currency and membership settings.

## Shared maintenance

Each community contributes collectively to hosting, service administration, software maintenance and development. The community organises its contribution and explains any arrangements affecting members.

The contribution is **0.1 HOUR per account per year**: a community with 100 accounts contributes 10 HOUR annually. HOUR is the reference unit used to compare community currencies. The number of exchanges does not affect the contribution.

This minimum contribution covers maintenance and development costs. It can be made in the community's own currency when external transfers are enabled. [Contact the platform operator](contact.md) to agree the arrangements. Changes will be communicated to affected communities in advance.

## Data processing agreement

The community's responsible organisation or identified representatives are the **data controller**, with primary responsibility for members' personal data. The community decides which data to collect, who can access it and how long to keep it. It must establish a lawful basis, inform members and handle their data-protection requests.

The **platform operator**, identified in the [Legal notice](terms-of-use.md), acts as **processor** for community data and follows the community's documented instructions to provide the service. It assists with members' data-protection requests, notifies the community of personal-data breaches, and returns or deletes data at the community's direction when hosting ends, subject to legal retention requirements. The [Privacy policy](privacy.md) describes the data processed and its visibility.
